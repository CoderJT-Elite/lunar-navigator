    How far to trust it

    Where the Sun and Earth are
    NASA/JPL Horizons, hourly, March 2025 to November 2030. We checked 600 random moments at ten sites against live Horizons. The worst elevation error was 0.00003°.
    What the skyline is
    NASA’s LRO laser-altimeter terrain at 80 m. The sunniest ridge point this tool found (−89.44°, 222.6°E) is lit 93% of 2027 with a 168-day unbroken stretch. Published work puts the best rim spot near 240 days, so ours is a less extreme case on the same ridge. We have not pinned down why.
    What it does not do
    Sunlight and line of sight only: no heat, dust or slope under the lander. Terrain exists south of 80°S, so every other site assumes a flat horizon. Antennas and arrays are taken as 2 m up.
