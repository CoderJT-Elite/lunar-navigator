# Lunar Landing Site Navigator

NASA Space Apps 2026, challenge: Lunar Landing Site Navigator. Team Fourteen Days Dark.

If a Moon landing slips, does the lander still get sunlight and a link to Earth? Compare sites and dates, see the real terrain skyline, and map how many days a landing can slip at every 1 km point near the south pole.

Data: JPL Horizons (Sun and Earth positions), NASA LRO LOLA south polar terrain. Limits: sunlight and line of sight only; terrain exists south of 80 S only. Written with AI assistance (Claude Code).
